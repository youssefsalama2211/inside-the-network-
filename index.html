05
Act V — The Defense

Every one of these is fixable.

No new budget. No new product. Configuration and hygiene the internal team already owns.

✓Kill anonymous SMB and apply least-privilege on every share.
✓Retire SMBv1 — keep SMB2/3 only.
✓Require SMB signing — "enabled" is not "enforced".
✓Lock down printers — authenticate remote access, restrict management, patch firmware.
✓Modernize TLS — drop SSLv3/RC4/3DES, replace the expired certificate.
✓Treat the LAN as hostile — an internal network is not a trusted zone.
